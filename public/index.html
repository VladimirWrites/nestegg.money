What it looks like

Encrypted in your browser

Your figures are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your account number. The server only ever stores ciphertext — it can't read a thing.

Net worth over time

Log your assets each year and watch your wealth grow, broken down by allocation. Stocks priced live, foreign currencies converted at the right historical rate.

Salary history

Track every month's net pay for you and your partner side by side, with raises and job changes marked, and a combined yearly trend.

Property & loans, done right

Mortgages and car loans with real amortization — extra payments, rate-fixed periods, depreciation — so the equity in your net worth is always accurate.

Synced, but yours

Pick up where you left off on any device. Everything syncs end-to-end encrypted — and Export JSON gives you a backup that's truly your own.

No account, no tracking

No email, no password, no profile. We never see who you are — just an opaque hash and an encrypted blob. Nothing to harvest, nothing to sell.

For developers & AI agents

99 finance calculators, as an MCP server

The same money math powers a free, open Model Context Protocol server and JSON API — loans & mortgages, time value of money, investing & risk, bonds, options, retirement, real estate, business, tax, and everyday money. Exact German net-salary math for 2023–2026 is built in, verified against the official BMF test tables. It's a stateless remote service — your inputs are used to compute a result and then discarded, never stored.

claude mcp add --transport http nestegg https://nestegg.money/mcp
